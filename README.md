# Half-Like 
A reskin collage for Hideous Destructor themed to the Half-Life Franchise Primarily, With Minor Cameos from other games and mods.
 
 
 A special thanks to ZikShadow for his model shot packs and Tommygalano5's City 17 sprite set respectively. 


Years ago near the end of 2021 when I first started playing HDest I wanted a spas-12 skin to reskin the hunter.
I eventually found TG5's WIP City 17 pack he posted earlier that year. later on i discovered zikshadows various skin sets and used 
the half life one for quite a while. 
eventually after accumulating more and more reskins and making a few more i had a situation where my load orders got corrupted by zdl
so i decided to combine them into one set to shrink my load order size and in the proccess started excercising more discretion in the various 
details included.
